offline acceptance ownership, original literal expectations, optional mapping

## Spec

descriptors, including the earlier Tagged Type3 descriptor. Declared W2 triples,
vertical origins, advances and MMType1 snapshots were verified against ISO 32000.

missing glyph. The resulting guard now requires exact agreement outside the
seven original one-pixel edge shells. A fresh independent public CLI probe
confirmed actual secondary rendering passes and secondary-only missing-glyph,
interior-hole and unrelated-mark defects fail. Its raw closure observations
are retained alongside this record.

The new uncertainty/geometry case was reviewed independently: its matrices,
spacing/TJ, saved/restored text state, separate rotation/UserUnit, four mapping
confidences, actual source space and ordered complete diagnostics are correct.
The reviewer did not derive expectations from Folio output. Incremental Spec
review ended CLEAN.
