# Combined extraction evidence qualification

The single-product `observe` command passed its first actual four-chain test,
retaining the exact marked-structure input and qualifying 334 declaration rules
plus 42 program predicates and their 165 controls. Its original missing-route
RED and first GREEN logs remain retained. The whole-corpus command then received
a separate public missing-route RED before implementation.

Independent read-only review of the first aggregation revision found two
remaining evidence-retention defects. The combined manifest omitted the raw
syntax, semantic and visual subtrees, and the whole-corpus manifest omitted
the detailed negative-control subtrees. It also established early product
report hashes only after every product had been created. Both issues required
producer-bound receipts before this aggregation could supply final certification.
The original passing single-product files remain unchanged: the archive and its
complete identities allow these omissions to be inspected without altering data.

The retained public RED/GREEN cycles now cover producer receipts for generation,
syntax, semantic capture, both raster observations, combined aggregation and
every negative-control batch. The secondary raster controls retain their
edge-only positive and missing-glyph, interior-hole and outside-ink negatives;
a replaced original raster reference is rejected against its frozen identity.

Both independent axes closed the two P1 findings by read-only source and
existing-result inspection. They checked 1,083 frozen identities and 2,722
retained file entries across eight manifests, without running a new CLI or
mutation probe. Their original reports are in `spec-retention-closure.tar.xz`
and `standards-retention-closure.tar.xz`. The original and closure source
snapshots are retained under `../recorder-r3/whole-retention-snapshot-archive-identities.json`;
compiled classes are identified there without redistribution.

`whole-retention-green.tar.xz` preserves all 19,700 files of the repeated actual
ten-product run, including its complete 19,698-entry root manifest and updated
negative controls. The archive recipe compares every retained byte with the
original. Its four aggregate chains pass; this is development qualification,
not final candidate or environment certification. The broader acceptance run
also exposed an existing T19 semantic caller budget that needs reconciliation
with newly counted Identity CMap data. Do not infer a passing full build from
the T13 results or the incremental review closure.

The completed broader run recorded 74 tests: 25 T13 recorder tests, 13 T13 tool
qualification tests and 12 shared standards tests passed; one of 24 older
acceptance tests failed because its T19 font-data budget was still 32. A public
Workflow-only repro against the original T19 PDF isolated that limit: increasing
the mapping or decoded-byte limits alone did not help. The T19 observer now
allows two complete 65,536-entry Identity CMap domains plus its former 32-entry
budget. All 24 acceptance tests then passed in the retained 55-second build.
This preserves the new Native accounting and adds no T19 product functionality.
