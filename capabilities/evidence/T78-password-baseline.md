Status: `compatible`

The [T78 certification profile](../../docs/t78-certification.md) freezes the
source-traceable version/algorithm/revision/filter/credential/permission table,
61 related Stable/Preview mappings, 68 original-byte public products and the
independently authored input corpus. Thirty-one Native, eleven Facade and two
artifact contracts must pass in each required tuple before recording.

Separate syntax, standards, semantic and visual chains must pass with all 95
security controls, nineteen core standards controls, wrong/missing credential
proofs, a truncated file, changed public observations/paint and an exact
one-pixel comparison. Every product is reopened. Randomized ciphertext hashes,
actual execution settings, source/contract/candidate/tool identities and raw
safe observations are retained and independently replayed by the collector.

The [Foundation evidence authority](../foundation-evidence.yaml) exclusively
binds current candidate receipts for Ubuntu 24.04 Linux x86-64 × JDK 8/11/17/21
× Native IN_PROCESS/HARDENED_WORKER. The Facade runs IN_PROCESS on every JDK;
its results are never relabeled Worker. This contract does not certify a
changed candidate. Windows/macOS remain uncertified and nonblocking for F0.1.0.

The parent aggregate remains experimental; #79 metadata-clear and #80
embedded-files-only remain required separate obligations. The historical T16
syntax-only evidence is unchanged. Compatible `document.value.inspect-patch`
is inherited as an external gate, and the aggregate is not a child prerequisite.
