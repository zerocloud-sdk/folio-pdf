# T76 image and Resource Inventory certification contract

Status: `compatible`

Capability: `document.images-resources.extract`

Acceptance Profile: `T14-image-resource-extraction`

Release train: `0.1.0-SNAPSHOT`

The normative [T14 certification contract](../../docs/t14-certification.md)
fixes three Stable/Preview mappings, five original products, literal metadata
and byte expectations, exact resource bounds and four independent chains.
Syntax and semantic observations cover ten Native/Facade products. Standards
and visual observations cover the eight conforming products; the separate
classification product preserves documented malformed/unsupported metadata.

Successful encoded extraction of real CCITT, JPEG, JPX, JBIG2 and LZW payloads,
independently rendered from their original bytes, resolves the recorded image
format blocker alongside successful unfiltered/ASCIIHex/ASCII85/RunLength/Flate
decoded paths and TIFF/PNG predictors. The explicit unsupported decoded and
external-data contracts remain. No broader image normalization, content paint
usage, inline-image extraction or page-raster API is asserted.

Each actual Ubuntu 24.04/Linux x86-64 JDK 8/11/17/21 × Native
IN_PROCESS/HARDENED_WORKER tuple must run 28 Native, eight Facade and two artifact
contracts, then the pinned independent recorder. Facade execution is always
recorded as IN_PROCESS. One hundred and two standards controls, seven semantic controls
and two visual controls must be detected. Tool identities, original subprocess
findings, complete manifests and source/artifact/environment identities are
required; changed or incomplete observations cannot pass.

The [Foundation evidence authority](../foundation-evidence.yaml) governs fresh
candidate certification and the refreshed predecessor obligations. The four
[syntax](T76-images-syntax.md), [standards](T76-images-standards.md),
[semantic](T76-images-semantic.md) and [visual](T76-images-visual.md) indexes
describe their separate evidence. They cannot pre-certify a changed candidate.
Windows and macOS remain explicitly uncertified.

The original [T14 implementation receipt](T14-image-resource-extraction.md)
and syntax-only receipt remain historical records with their original claims.
Issue #76 adds certification through public interfaces; no Native backend
implementation, downstream Forms/Trust slice or release publication is included.
