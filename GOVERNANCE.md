# Governance

During incubation, the project founder under ZeroCloud SDK acts as Lead
Maintainer and has final responsibility for merges, releases, accepted ADRs,
Capability Matrix status, and clean-room review. The temporary public contact
is **mabaiqiu@gmail.com**. Governance may move to a maintainer council when
sustained contribution makes that useful.

All contributions require a Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off and a provenance statement covering authorship, reference materials, test fixtures, and dependency licenses. A contribution that cannot establish clean provenance is not eligible for merge.

Legally approved black-box comparisons of closed Reference Suite products may only be performed by a Compatibility Curator who is separate from the corresponding implementers. That role is currently vacant; until it is staffed, closed-product differential evidence is unavailable and affected work remains `planned`. Once staffed, implementers receive only neutral Acceptance Profiles and project-owned fixtures, while the curator's evidence and authorization are retained for audit.
