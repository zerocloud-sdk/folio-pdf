# Require independent Acceptance Evidence chains

Acceptance Profiles combine independent syntax evidence, standards validation, project-owned semantic and structural assertions, and visual evidence from a renderer independent of the implementation; accessibility profiles also retain required human review. qpdf, veraPDF, PDFium-based rendering, and versioned corpora are isolated acceptance tools rather than runtime dependencies. Legally approved iText black-box differences may discover migration gaps but never serve as the golden output or correctness authority.
